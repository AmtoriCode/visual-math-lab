How do we solve ∫x² dx?

∫ x² dx = x³/3 + C

See integration as accumulated area, derive the power rule, understand the constant of integration, and verify the result by differentiation.

Reverse the power rule

Raise the exponent from 2 to 3 and divide by the new exponent.

Differentiate to verify

d/dx(x³/3+C)=(1/3)(3x²)+0=x².
