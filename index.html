The idea in three moves

1 — Accumulate areaThe integral tracks area under the parabola y = x². From 0 to a, that area is a³/3.
2 — Use the power ruleIncrease the exponent from 2 to 3, then divide by the new exponent: x³/3.
3 — Differentiate to checkd/dx(x³/3 + C) = (1/3)(3x²) + 0 = x².
